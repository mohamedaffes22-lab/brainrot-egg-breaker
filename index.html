FAQ

Questions before you break your first egg

The short answers to what people ask most about the brainrot-egg-breaker script.

Is the Break a Brainrot Egg Script free? +
Yes. brainrot-egg-breaker is free and open source. Every build is published on gitscore.cc, and the source is public on GitHub if you want to review or compile it yourself.
Does instant hatch actually speed up egg breaking? +
It removes the hatch animation wait between breaks and re-queues the next egg immediately. In practice that means several times more hatches per session compared to clicking manually.
How does the rare mutation sniper work? +
The script reads roll results as they appear and compares them against your selected mutation list. When something matches, it flags the hatch and keeps a timestamped log so you can review the run later.
Can I leave the AFK farm loop running overnight? +
That's exactly what it's designed for. Set the loop limits and mutation filters, keep your machine awake, and it will keep breaking and collecting in the background. Check the log in the morning.
